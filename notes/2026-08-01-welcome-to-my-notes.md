Why keep notes in public? Because the best way to learn something is to explain
it — and the best way to remember a fix is to write it down while it's fresh.

This is my running notebook on the way from **Infrastructure Engineer to CEO**:
root causes, migration checklists, scripts that saved me hours, and short posts
on things I wish I'd known sooner.

## How these posts are published

Every post here is a plain **Markdown file** committed to the repository, listed
in a small `notes/posts.json` index. That means:

- The blog is **public and versioned** — every post lives in git history.
- There's **no backend, no database, and no login** — it's pure GitHub Pages.
- Nothing a visitor types can change what you're reading. Posts only change when
  I commit a file.

## What to expect

- Real troubleshooting write-ups from Azure VMware Solution, migrations, backup
  and DR, and automation.
- Short `code` snippets and checklists I actually reuse.
- The occasional lesson learned the hard way.
